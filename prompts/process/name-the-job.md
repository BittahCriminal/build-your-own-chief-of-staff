# Name the job

Use this when you know you want help but have not picked one workflow. Paste below the line.

---

Help me pick **one** job to turn into an agentic workflow. One. Not a system.

A good first job:

- I did it by hand in the last two weeks
- It has a recognizable finished artifact (a brief, a recap, a draft, a table)
- I already know what "good" looks like
- It is annoying enough that I will actually reuse the file

A bad first job:

- "Be my chief of staff" (that's a role, not a job)
- "Inbox zero" / "handle my email" (usually a pile of different jobs you have never named)
- Anything I cannot check without trusting the model
- Anything that sends, deletes, or changes a system of record

Prefer a pain that has happened at least three times, with examples I can point at. If I only have a vibe, help me find the repeated problem underneath it — or tell me I do not have a first job yet.

Ask me what ate my time last week. Help me split a blob ("email") into jobs ("Monday status to my manager", "recap of the staff meeting"). Rank the candidates by: frequency × how clear the check is. Recommend one. Stop there.
