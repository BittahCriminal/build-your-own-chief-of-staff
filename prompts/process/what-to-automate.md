# What to automate (when you freeze)

Paste everything below the line. Use this when you have an agent and no idea what to give it — or when “automate my job” is too big to start.

---

You are helping me unstick. I am staring at a capable agent and I do not know what to hand it. Do not build a workflow yet. Do not write a prompt file yet. Do not pick a vendor. Do not let the model choose a problem *and* start building it.

The failure I am trying to avoid: teams that start at the **core** of the work (the judgment, the craft, the hard call) stall. Teams that start at the **edges** (prep, check, package, handoff) ship. A blob like “handle my email” is usually many jobs pretending to be one.

Interview me. One question at a time. Wait for my answer. Do not guess my week.

Walk this sequence, skipping any step I have already answered clearly:

1. **Last week, not the fantasy.** What ate time last week — a thing I actually did, not a thing I wish were automated.
2. **Split the blob.** If I name a pile (“email,” “meetings,” “status”), help me split it into finished artifacts (Monday update, recap, ask, bad-news note). One pile is not one job.
3. **Edge or core?** For each candidate, is it:
   - **Edge:** data prep, checking, summarizing, packaging, moving information between places — mechanical, around the real work.
   - **Core:** hard judgment, taste, relationships, red lines — errors cascade.
4. **The two tests** on the edges only:
   - Repeatable: same steps next week?
   - Verifiable: a human can check it against a source, a template, or a yes/no rule?
5. **Fast-win filter.** Among those that pass both tests, pick the *simplest* edge with the clearest lift — not the most impressive. Prefer something I can try in days, draft-only. Three real occurrences beats a vibe. Frequency is evidence, not proof it is worth building.
6. **Choose none is allowed.** If nothing passes, say so. Do not invent a job to fill the silence.

Finish with this block, nothing else:

```
Last week (the actual work):
Jobs I split out:
Edges (candidates):
Core (keep human):
Fast win (one):
  Repeatable: yes/no — why
  Verifiable: yes/no — why
  Check I will use:
Verdict: automate this edge / keep as judgment / split further / choose none
If automate: the draft-only first run (what I will paste, what I will not let it send)
```

If I am vague, ask the next powerful question. Do not start solving the job. Do not offer five automations and start coding the winner.
