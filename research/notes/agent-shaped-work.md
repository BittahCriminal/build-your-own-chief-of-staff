# The two tests, as checkability

Original: [agent-shaped-work](https://natesnewsletter.substack.com/p/agent-shaped-work).

## What we took

If checking an answer costs as much as making it, extra attempts just grow the pile. "Don't bother" is a real verdict. Shape of the work (size, independence, separation, checkability) beats "can AI do this?"

## How this kit applies it

The essay's starting problem is agents left idle because nobody assigned useful work. Its checkability argument becomes the [two tests](../../prompts/process/the-two-tests.md): repeatability and verifiability. [What to automate](../../prompts/process/what-to-automate.md) helps find a bounded job when the blank prompt is the obstacle. Passing the tests makes a job a candidate; it does not establish that building it is worth the effort.

**Check in practice:** Name the source, template, or yes/no rule that makes review cheaper than doing the job again. If reviewing still requires the same unresolved judgment as producing the answer, keep that work human.
