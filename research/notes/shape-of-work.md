# Shape of the work, not "can AI"

Original: [build-buy-hire-wait-ai-matrix](https://natesnewsletter.substack.com/p/build-buy-hire-wait-ai-matrix).

## What we took

Classify the work first (how often, cost of a mistake, how much judgment). The five-axis score in `score-the-candidates.md` operationalizes this ranking without bypassing the human choice: frequency, blast radius, and the two tests come from this classification; spec readiness is a kit addition tied to `sources.md`. Automating judgment-heavy work breaks the process where the human mattered.

## How this kit applies it

Use the [decision matrix](../../prompts/process/score-the-candidates.md) to compare three or more candidates. It scores repeatability, verifiability, frequency, blast radius, and spec readiness. The numerical scale, verdict thresholds, and source-readiness axis are kit design choices; they should not be attributed to the original executive matrix.

**Check in practice:** A zero on repeatability or verifiability overrides the total. Review the evidence behind the other scores, then let the person choose which job, if any, to pursue.
