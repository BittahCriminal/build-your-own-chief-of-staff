# Delegation Kit → Chief of Staff

Original: [natesnewsletter / grab-the-delegation-kit](https://natesnewsletter.substack.com/p/grab-the-delegation-kit-i-use-to).

## What we took

Coping does not transfer. The bottleneck is specifying work. Eight portable jobs: clarifier, translation, task spec, daily brief, meeting processing, weekly review, end-of-day, memory scaffold. Spec needs inputs, steps, constraints, approvals at *reversibility* boundaries, failure handling. Score: can a stranger execute it; is success binary. Judgment (taste, relationships, red lines) stays human.

## How this kit applies it

Use [Name the job](../../prompts/process/name-the-job.md) to turn an intention into one assignment, then [Write the SOP](../../prompts/process/write-the-sop.md) to state its inputs, steps, output, and check. Record constraints, where the agent must stop, and how missing inputs are handled. The [workflow prompts](../../prompts/workflows/) demonstrate operational assistance; decisions about relationships and judgment stay with the person.

**Check in practice:** A person unfamiliar with the job can follow the specification and tell whether the result passes. A brief, recap, or draft is the deliverable; sending it is a separate human action.
