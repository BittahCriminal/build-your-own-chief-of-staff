# Let history pick — then you choose

Original: [let-ai-pick-what-to-automate](https://natesnewsletter.substack.com/p/let-ai-pick-what-to-automate).

## What we took

Frequency is evidence, not value. Do not let the model pick the problem *and* build it. Offer sheet of evidenced options; choosing **none** is allowed. Only then a bounded build.

## How this kit applies it

Use [What to automate](../../prompts/process/what-to-automate.md) to make recurring friction visible and [Score the candidates](../../prompts/process/score-the-candidates.md) when several jobs remain. The source describes abandoning a combined discovery-and-build experiment because selecting the problem needs its own human decision. An offer should explain both the evidence of repetition and why removing that work would help.

**Check in practice:** The person can inspect the evidence, compare the options, and choose none before any build begins. A high event count alone does not establish value.

## What we left out

Model bakeoff (Fable vs Codex) as a product recommendation.
