# Why agent projects keep failing

Original: [why-your-ai-agents-keep-failing](https://natesnewsletter.substack.com/p/why-your-ai-agents-keep-failing-and).

## What we took

Start at the edges (prep, QA, synthesis, packaging, coordination), not the core. Core-first → stalled agents and bloated scope. Fastest, clearest lift — not the most impressive. Semi-manual v0. Trust is the real project.

## How this kit applies it

[What to automate](../../prompts/process/what-to-automate.md) separates support tasks from core judgment. [Diagnose the stall](../../prompts/process/diagnose-the-stall.md) revisits scope when an ambitious workflow stops progressing. The first useful version can take pasted inputs and produce a manually checked draft; a complete integration is not a prerequisite.

**Check in practice:** The pilot has one bounded deliverable, a review the person can perform, and a plausible reduction in recurring effort. Expand scope only after that edge task becomes dependable.
