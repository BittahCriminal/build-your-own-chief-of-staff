# Automation Discovery

Original: [unlock-ai / automation-discovery](https://unlock-ai.natebjones.com/guides/automation-discovery).

## What we took

Three independent occurrences before a candidate counts. "Nothing worth building" is a valid answer. Human gates: approve sources, then choose the offer.

## How this kit applies it

Use independent examples when discovering candidates with [What to automate](../../prompts/process/what-to-automate.md). Three messages in one busy thread are one occurrence, not three. Ask what is already automated before treating activity as manual work. The user names available sources in a private [sources file](../../context-templates/sources.md) and chooses a job before moving to [Write the SOP](../../prompts/process/write-the-sop.md).

**Check in practice:** Each candidate has evidence from at least three separate occurrences. Thin history, existing coverage, or a lack of worthwhile options can end the exercise without a build.
