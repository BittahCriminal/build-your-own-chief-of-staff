# Verification gap

Original: [verification field notes](https://natesnewsletter.substack.com/p/my-honest-field-notes-on-the-verification).

## What we took

"Done" is a contract, not a vibe. Self-reported completion is structurally unreliable. Translate taste into checkable constraints. Competitive reviews, recaps, postmortems: the machine enforces the floor; the human owns the ceiling. If you cannot name "not yet," do not automate.

## How this kit applies it

Write the [SOP's check](../../prompts/process/write-the-sop.md) before the first run, then use [Verify](../../prompts/process/verify.md) against the resulting artifact. Separate mechanical requirements, such as complete sections and supporting sources, from the human judgment needed to accept a recommendation.

**Check in practice:** Give a concrete example of an output that would be rejected and the evidence that would make it acceptable. The agent's statement that it finished is not evidence.
