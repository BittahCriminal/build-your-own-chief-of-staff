# A better model will not save you

Original: [chatgpt-5-wont-save-you](https://natesnewsletter.substack.com/p/chatgpt-5-wont-save-you-10-reasons).

## What we took

Failures are data, job-shaping, ownership, and ops — not missing intelligence. Waiting for the next release is how projects die.

## How this kit applies it

Treat model-upgrade promises as insufficient explanations for a stalled workflow. [Diagnose the stall](../../prompts/process/diagnose-the-stall.md) asks about the job, source of truth, authority boundary, check, and owner. [Write the SOP](../../prompts/process/write-the-sop.md) makes those decisions concrete enough to try with the tool the user already has.

**Check in practice:** Identify a specific process failure and a small repair that can be evaluated on a real run. Waiting for another release does not resolve missing inputs, vague success criteria, or absent ownership.

## What we left out

Boardroom / enterprise-procurement framing.
