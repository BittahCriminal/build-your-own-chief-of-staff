# Reusable rig — drafts, never sends

Original: [reusable-ai-agent](https://natesnewsletter.substack.com/p/reusable-ai-agent).

## What we took

One rig, many nouns. Citation guard: no anchor, no claim. The gate: draft and organize only; never send, file, submit, pay, or sign. Each job should make the next cheaper.

## How this kit applies it

Reuse the same [process](../../PROCESS.md) across briefs, meeting notes, and email drafts. Keep context, sources, and instructions in inspectable files that work across providers. The [verification prompt](../../prompts/process/verify.md) checks support for claims; the [workflow prompts](../../prompts/workflows/) stop at drafts. A later job benefits from the context and corrections already captured.

**Check in practice:** Every substantive claim points to supporting material the reviewer can open. The run hands back a draft and visible gaps without sending, submitting, paying, signing, or changing external records.
