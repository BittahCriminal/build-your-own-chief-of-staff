# False success / Mission Fit

Original: [ai-agent-false-success](https://natesnewsletter.substack.com/p/ai-agent-false-success).

## What we took

`done` is a claim about the world. A plausible substitute plus a matching filename is how people stop checking. Describe the result without the word “done.” Proof from the system of record, not the transcript. Access / quality / evidence / supervision.

## How this kit applies it

The source's example is an email draft with the expected attachment name but the wrong spreadsheet content. [Verify](../../prompts/process/verify.md) applies the lesson by examining the artifact and its evidence. [Schedule it](../../prompts/process/schedule-it.md) extends it to recurring work: a chat assertion is not proof that a routine exists in the scheduler.

**Check in practice:** Describe the required result precisely, open it in the system that owns it, and compare it with the requested source and content. If access is missing, report that gap; do not substitute a plausible artifact and call it complete.
