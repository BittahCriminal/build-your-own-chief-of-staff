# Every agent needs an owner

Original: [ai-agent-ownership](https://natesnewsletter.substack.com/p/ai-agent-ownership).

## What we took

One accountable owner, not a committee. Ownerless agents fail quietly (stale diet, rotted instructions, dead review).

## How this kit applies it

The [process](../../PROCESS.md#6-correct-the-file-not-the-chat) assigns one person to notice drift and maintain the job. Ownership includes knowing which sources are current, whether the output is being reviewed, and when the job should change or stop. [Schedule it](../../prompts/process/schedule-it.md) keeps an owner attached when a verified job becomes recurring.

**Check in practice:** Name the person who reviews the work and can correct or pause it. Shared access is not a substitute for that responsibility.
