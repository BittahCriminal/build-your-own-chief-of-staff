# Fails at the task level

Original: [decompose workflows](https://natesnewsletter.substack.com/p/grab-the-10-prompts-i-use-to-decompose).

## What we took

AI fails at the *task*, not the workflow. Most workflows are five or six jobs pretending to be one. “Which model?” is the wrong first question.

## How this kit applies it

Split a broad request such as handling email into distinct activities: gather context, identify the ask, draft, review, and send. The kit supports the draft work and leaves sending with the person. [What to automate](../../prompts/process/what-to-automate.md) and [Name the job](../../prompts/process/name-the-job.md) make those boundaries explicit before an SOP is written.

**Check in practice:** Each candidate has one recognizable output and its own check. If its score changes depending on which part you mean, split it further before choosing a tool.
