# Where the agent should stop

Original: [first-ai-agent-use-case](https://natesnewsletter.substack.com/p/first-ai-agent-use-case).

## What we took

Start where the customer (or colleague) already tells you you're wrong. Reconstructing context is the expensive part; the reply is cheap. Draft-only until you can check.

## How this kit applies it

Choose a first job whose output a colleague or customer can correct against known facts. The useful assistance is often collecting the scattered context needed for an answer. [Meeting prep](../../prompts/workflows/meeting-prep.md) and [Draft email](../../prompts/workflows/draft-email.md) use that division: gather the evidence and prepare the artifact, then hand it back.

**Check in practice:** The person can check the reconstructed context and proposed response before anything is sent or changed. Repeated repairs may reveal a process problem that should be fixed at its source.
