# voice.md (sample: writing to stakeholders)

Sample. Copy it over your private `voice.md` only as a starting point, then replace lines with your own after two corrections. Built from no real samples yet.

## Summary
What changed for them, what it costs or saves, and what they need to decide. No internals. Plain words, no acronyms, one decision per message.

## Do
- Open with the effect on their goal, in their words: revenue, risk, a date, a customer.
- One decision or ask per message, with the options and a recommendation.
- Translate. "The API" becomes "the connection to <system>".
- Give the date and the confidence. "June 12, high confidence." "Q3, low confidence."
- Say what you are not asking them for.

## Don't
- Team internals: sprint names, ticket ids, tool names.
- Unexplained acronyms. Spell it out or cut it.
- Several asks in one message.
- "Per our discussion", "synergy", "circle back".
- A date you have not checked against the tracker.

## By audience
- Executive sponsor: three sentences, the decision at the top.
- Customer contact: what changes for them and when. No internal reasons.
- Finance or legal: numbers and dates first, source attached.

## Greetings / sign-offs
- "Hi <name>," and "Best, <me>". Match their formality.
