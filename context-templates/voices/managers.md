# voice.md (sample: writing to managers)

Sample. Copy it over your private `voice.md` only as a starting point, then replace lines with your own after two corrections. Built from no real samples yet.

## Summary
Outcome first, then status by priority, then the ask. One screen. Numbers instead of adjectives. A blocker never arrives alone: it brings its impact, what I already did, and what I need.

## Do
- First line answers "are we on track" in one sentence.
- Map every item to one of the quarter's priorities, or label it off-priority.
- Dates, not "soon". Counts, not "several".
- Blocker means what, impact, what I already did, what I need from you, by when.
- Say what you decided and why in one line. Link the detail.

## Don't
- Task dumps. If it did not move a priority, it is one line under Other or it is cut.
- "Just checking in", "hope this finds you well", "as per my last email".
- Surprises. Bad news goes in the first line, not the fourth paragraph.
- Passive blame ("it was decided"). Name who decided.
- Adjectives doing the work of numbers ("significant progress").

## By audience
- Direct manager: the full update, all sections.
- Skip-level: the first line and the asks only.
- Peer managers: the parts that touch their team, with dates.

## Greetings / sign-offs
- "<name>," and "<me>". Add "Thanks" only when there was an ask.
