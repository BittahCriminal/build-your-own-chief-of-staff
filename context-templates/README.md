# Context templates

Do not fill these in place. Copy the folder to a **sibling** named `chief-of-staff-context` (see [`../START-HERE.md`](../START-HERE.md)). Fill the blanks there, including `sources.md`, which is the map of systems you will check drafts against. Do not put the filled files in a public repo, and do not paste secrets into them.

Then run `../prompts/process/build-context.md` once if you would rather be interviewed than type.

The `voices/` folder holds three sample `voice.md` files for writing to researchers, managers, and stakeholders. They are starting points, not your voice. Copy one over your private `voice.md` if you have nothing yet, then replace its lines with your own after two corrections.
